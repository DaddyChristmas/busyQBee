railkeep

1960s–1970s Analogue Railway Tycoon & Tactile Locomotive Operations

A 2D top-down railway management and operations tycoon. Merging the tactile, physics-driven locomotive controls of heavy freight operations with macro logistics expansion. No abstracted point-and-click dispatching—every switch is thrown and every train is manually driven.

  • ♦
    Tactile Cab Controls: Authentic notch throttle, mechanical reverser, independent locomotive brake, and realistic train air pipe charging (70–90 PSI).
  • ♦
    Momentum & Hazard Physics: Realistic freight tonnage physics, downhill brake management, wheel slip on wet iron, and risk of derailments.
  • ♦
    1960s–1970s Analogue Fidelity: EMD/ALCO style diesel-electric switchers, riveted rolling stock, mechanical semaphores, and carbon-copy waybills.

Rachel

Cold War SIGINT Radar Intercept & Black Site Noir

You are the Operations Director of Site K-9 ("Halcyon Flats"), a classified desert installation operating between 1955 and 1989. By day, intercept adversary flights on tactile phosphor radar scopes, verify suspect dossiers across rotary phones, and unmask foreign infiltration. By night, walk the floodlit tarmac alone and uncover what was unearthed beneath Hangar 4.

  • ♦
    Tactile Workload Multi-Tasking: Concurrent procedural workflows—ticking darkroom chemical timers, zero-beating radio frequencies, and real-time radar tracks.
  • ♦
    Luxurious Retro Noir Aesthetic: Volumetric sodium floodlights, humming CRT phosphors, Bakelite dials, and ominous desert shadows.
  • ♦
    Counter-Espionage Deduction: Synthesize fragmented telemetry, weed out disinformation, and shape branching Cold War geopolitical outcomes.

Core Tenets

The busyQBee Standard

Mobile titles deserve genuine physical simulation, nuanced tactile inputs, and deep noir atmosphere.

⚙

Tactile Mechanical Depth

No shallow quick-time events. We simulate real brake pipes, throttle notches, cargo swing physics, and radio frequencies so every interaction feels mechanical and deliberate.

✨

Noir Cartoony Lighting

Atmosphere defines our visual identity. High-contrast ink lines, 2D CanvasTexture normal mapping, volumetric sodium streetlights, and moody phosphor CRT glows.

⚡

Mobile-First Performance

Engineered with decoupled architecture and native engine optimizations in Godot, ensuring rock-solid 60 FPS performance, low thermal footprint, and zero battery drain.
