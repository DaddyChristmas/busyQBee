Rachel Privacy Policy

Game: Rachel (Cold War SIGINT Noir Simulation) Status: Zero Personal Data Collected Effective: November 14, 2026
Privacy Policy Terms of Service (ToS) Studio Policy
Zero-Collection Architecture: While Site K-9 investigates classified SIGINT intercepts and security dockets in-universe, busyQBee Studios intercepts none of your personal information. Rachel operates strictly offline on your local client.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy outlines data handling for the interactive noir simulation Rachel, created by busyQBee Studios.

2. Local Storage Only

All gameplay dossiers, branching narrative choices, interrogation notes, and radar telemetry deciphering records remain exclusively on your device storage:

  • Story branch milestones and investigation dockets.
  • CRT phosphor display calibrations and audio mixer presets.
  • Discovered black-budget airframe archives.

3. No Personal Data Collected

Rachel contains zero personal data harvesting mechanisms:

  • No player surveillance, device fingerprinting, or advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics beacons, location monitoring, or third-party ad networks.
  • No selling, trading, or sharing of personal data under any circumstances.

4. Children's Privacy

Rachel is designed for mature tactical audiences and does not gather information from any users, including children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).
