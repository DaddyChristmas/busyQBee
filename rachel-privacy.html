Rachel Privacy Policy

Game: Rachel (Cold War SIGINT Noir Simulation) Effective Date: November 14, 2026 Developer: busyQBee Studios
Privacy Policy Terms of Service (ToS) Studio Policy
Privacy Commitment: While Site K-9 deals in classified surveillance and intelligence interception, busyQBee Studios respects your actual personal privacy. We collect zero personal surveillance data on our players.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy outlines the data handling practices of busyQBee Studios regarding the interactive espionage and radar simulation game Rachel.

2. Data Collection & Storage

A. Local Narrative Progress & Investigation Dossiers

All gameplay intelligence files, branching story decisions, suspect interrogation logs, and unlocked black-budget airframe records are saved locally on your client device:

  • Act and narrative branch progression states.
  • Radar intercept records and audio telemetry deciphering logs.
  • Director's desk preferences, CRT phosphor shader configurations, and audio levels.

B. Anonymous Diagnostics & Crash Prevention

To ensure stable rendering across complex 2D normal mapping and multi-channel audio synthesis, we may collect aggregated diagnostic data:

  • Crash reports and unhandled exception traces.
  • Device hardware model, OS revision, and GPU shader model support.
  • Aggregated performance metrics to benchmark loading times and memory usage.

3. Advertising & Tracking Prohibition

Rachel is designed as an uncompromising atmospheric experience. We do not incorporate third-party ad networks, location trackers, cross-site cookies, or commercial user profiling.

4. Children’s Privacy

Rachel is aimed at mature tactical and narrative puzzle audiences. We do not knowingly gather any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).

5. User Rights & Data Control

You can erase all game records at any time by clearing application data or deleting the game from your hardware. Inquiries regarding statutory rights under GDPR, CCPA, or other data protection regulations may be directed to our privacy contact below.
