railkeep Privacy Policy

Game: railkeep (Analogue Railway Operations Tycoon) Status: Zero Personal Data Collected Effective: November 14, 2026
Privacy Policy Terms of Service (ToS) Studio Policy
Zero-Collection Architecture: In keeping with our 1960s–1970s analogue philosophy, railkeep operates without online surveillance. All career rosters, train manifests, and brake physics states are kept strictly on your local hardware.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy outlines data handling for railkeep, developed by busyQBee Studios.

2. Local Storage Only

The game stores simulation and career progression entirely on your local device:

  • Yard marshalling progress, track junctions, and locomotive fleet rosters.
  • Hazard certifications, carbon-copy waybills, and financial ledgers.
  • Simulation settings, cab control calibrations, and audio volume levels.

3. No Personal Data Collected

railkeep does not collect, record, or transmit personal data:

  • No player names, emails, phone numbers, or social media logins.
  • No user tracking cookies, ad identifiers, or behavioral profilers.
  • No selling, trading, or sharing of user records with third parties.

4. Children's Privacy

railkeep does not collect data from any players, fully complying with COPPA, GDPR-K, and international privacy guidelines.
