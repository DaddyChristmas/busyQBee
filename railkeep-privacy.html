Privacy Policy for railkeep

Game: railkeep (Analogue Railway Operations Tycoon) Platform: Mobile Last updated: November 2026
Privacy Policy Terms of Service (ToS) Studio Policy
Zero Data Collection: railkeep is an offline single-player railway management and operations tycoon. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

1. Information Collection and Use

railkeep does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information, device identifiers, or usage analytics:

  • No user accounts or sign-ins are required.
  • No advertising or third-party analytics SDKs are included.
  • No network, location, contacts, camera, or sensor data is transmitted off your device.

2. Local Save Data & App Sandbox

All yard marshalling progression, freight waybills, rolling stock rosters, and cab settings are stored strictly locally in your device's private app sandbox. This data is never sent to external servers and is removed when you uninstall the game.

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Because the app collects zero personal data, it fully complies with COPPA, GDPR-K, and global child privacy regulations.

4. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this policy, contact us at: kai@sunraypm.com
