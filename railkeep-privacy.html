railkeep Privacy Policy

Game: railkeep (Analogue Railway Operations Tycoon) Effective Date: November 14, 2026 Developer: busyQBee Studios
Privacy Policy Terms of Service (ToS) Studio Policy
Privacy Commitment: railkeep is built with player privacy at its core. We do not harvest personal data, sell consumer profiles, or track you across the internet.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how busyQBee Studios manages data associated with railkeep, our upcoming tactile railway management and locomotive simulation game.

2. Data Collection Practices

A. Local Simulation & Career State

railkeep stores operational states locally on your device storage:

  • Yard marshalling progress, track layouts, and rolling stock rosters.
  • Locomotive certifications, debt balance, waybill logs, and freight delivery records.
  • Simulation settings, audio preferences, and control calibrations.

B. Diagnostics & Physics Performance Telemetry

During development, closed playtesting, and public release, we may collect aggregated and anonymized physics simulation metrics:

  • Frame timing statistics across heavy multi-consist track simulations.
  • Derailment and collision exception logs to debug physics solver anomalies.
  • Device operating system version, CPU architecture, and graphics hardware profile.

3. Third-Party Integrations

railkeep may integrate with official platform services (Google Play Games, Apple Game Center, Steamworks) exclusively to facilitate achievements, cloud save backups, and platform-level verification. We do not embed ad-tracking networks or behavioral analytics trackers.

4. Children’s Privacy

railkeep does not collect personally identifiable information from any players, including children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).

5. Data Retention & Rights

You have complete authority over your local game data. Deleting the application removes all local career records and caches. For cloud save purges or privacy queries under GDPR or CCPA, reach out to our team at support@busyqbee.com.
