Core Philosophy: At busyQBee Studios, we build captivating tactile games—not data harvesters. We do not sell personal information, and we minimize data collection across all our titles.

1. Overview & Game-Specific Policies

This general privacy policy sets out how busyQBee Studios treats information collected through our official website and mobile game catalog. For detailed terms tailored to each specific title, please review the individual game policies:

  • copterCove Privacy Policy (Sunblade Shores: Island Flight)
  • railkeep Privacy Policy (Analogue Railway Tycoon)
  • Rachel Privacy Policy (Cold War SIGINT Noir Simulation)

2. Website Information Collection

Our official website (busyqbee.github.io) is hosted statically via GitHub Pages.

  • We do not place tracking cookies or advertising pixels on your browser.
  • Standard server access logs (such as IP address, user-agent string, and requested page timestamp) are processed automatically by GitHub Inc. for security, threat prevention, and network operations in accordance with the GitHub Privacy Statement.

3. Communication & Support Requests

When you voluntarily email us at support@busyqbee.com regarding support, beta feedback, or press queries, we use your email address solely to correspond with you and address your inquiry. We never share your contact details with marketers or unauthorized third parties.

4. GDPR, UK-GDPR & CCPA Rights

If you reside in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, California, or other jurisdictions with data protection laws:

  • You have the right to request access to any personal data held about you.
  • You have the right to request erasure ("Right to be Forgotten") of your records.
  • You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

To exercise any rights, simply email our data protection contact below.
