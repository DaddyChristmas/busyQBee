copterCove Privacy Policy

Game: copterCove (Sunblade Shores: Island Flight) Status: Zero Personal Data Collected Effective: November 14, 2026
Privacy Policy Terms of Service (ToS) Studio Policy
Zero-Collection Architecture: busyQBee Studios does not collect, record, track, or sell your personal information. copterCove functions entirely on-device with no user account registration, no telemetry surveillance, and no behavioral tracking.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains the data privacy posture of copterCove (also distributed under the title Sunblade Shores: Island Flight), created by busyQBee Studios.

2. Local Storage Only

All gameplay data remains strictly on your device:

  • Unlocked islands, helipads, and flight routes.
  • In-game trade credits, cargo delivery scores, and flight logs.
  • Helicopter engine upgrades, winch modifications, and touch sensitivity preferences.

We do not operate remote servers to harvest this data. If you uninstall the application, all local save data is removed.

3. No Personal Data Collected

Because our game does not require user accounts, we do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses.
  • GPS or coarse geographic location.
  • Device identifiers for tracking or advertising.
  • Contact lists, microphone, or camera access.

Since we collect no personal data, there are no databases to breach, no profiles to sell, and no third-party tracking cookies.

4. Platform Cloud Services (Optional)

If you choose to use built-in mobile OS backup features (such as Apple iCloud or Google Play Backup), your device manages those backups directly through your personal Apple or Google account. busyQBee Studios has no access to your platform credentials or personal account profiles.

5. Children's Privacy

copterCove is safe and family-friendly for players of all ages. Because we do not collect personal information from any user, we naturally comply with COPPA, GDPR-K, and global child safety standards.
