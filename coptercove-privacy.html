copterCove Privacy Policy

Game: copterCove (Sunblade Shores: Island Flight) Effective Date: November 14, 2026 Developer: busyQBee Studios
Privacy Policy Terms of Service (ToS) Studio Policy
Privacy Commitment: busyQBee Studios does not sell your personal data. We collect only minimal diagnostic and save game data necessary to run, optimize, and improve the copterCove mobile experience.

1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how busyQBee Studios ("busyQBee", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you install, play, or access copterCove (also distributed under the title Sunblade Shores: Island Flight) on mobile and other supported operating systems.

By downloading or playing copterCove, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue play and uninstall the game.

2. Information We Collect

We are committed to data minimization. We only collect information strictly required to maintain gameplay functionality:

A. Local Gameplay and Save Progression

copterCove stores game save states directly on your device. This data includes:

  • Unlocked islands, helipads, and flight routes.
  • In-game currency, cargo mission completions, and flight logs.
  • Helicopter modifications, winch upgrades, and customized control preferences (e.g. inverted axis, touch sensitivity).

B. Cloud Saves and Platform Services

If you choose to link your device to platform services (such as Google Play Games or Apple Game Center), certain gameplay milestones, high scores, and save state backups may be synchronized to your personal platform account in accordance with Google's or Apple's respective privacy policies.

C. Anonymous Technical & Diagnostic Data

To ensure optimal frame rates (60 FPS) and eliminate crashes across diverse mobile hardware, we may collect aggregated, non-personally identifiable diagnostic metrics:

  • Device model, OS version, GPU family, and display resolution.
  • Crash call stacks, rendering pipeline fallbacks, and performance benchmarks.
  • General locale (country/language code) for localization optimization.

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

We believe in clean, respectful mobile entertainment:

  • We do not collect real names, physical addresses, or phone numbers.
  • We do not access your contacts, camera, or personal microphone.
  • We do not track your precise GPS coordinates.
  • We do not sell or trade your data with data brokers or behavioral advertising networks.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

copterCove is designed for general audiences of all ages, offering a cozy, family-friendly flight simulation. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain European jurisdictions).

If a parent or guardian believes their child has provided personal information to us without parental consent, please contact us at support@busyqbee.com. We will promptly delete such information.

5. Data Retention & Security

Local game save files remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the application. Diagnostic logs are aggregated, anonymized, and retained only for as long as necessary to resolve bugs and performance bottlenecks. We implement standard encryption and access safeguards to prevent unauthorized data access.

6. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

Depending on your location, you hold specific statutory rights regarding personal data:

  • Right to Know / Access: You may request disclosure of any personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Deletion: You may request the deletion of your cloud save or account records.
  • Right to Rectification: You may correct inaccuracies in your data.
  • Right to Opt-Out: Because we do not sell your personal data or engage in targeted ad tracking, we do not require a separate opt-out mechanism.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect new game updates, engine features, or legal requirements. When changes are made, the "Effective Date" at the top of this document will be updated. Continued play of copterCove following notice constitutes acceptance.
